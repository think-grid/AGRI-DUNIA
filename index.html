Government Schemes for Farmers

Text-to-Speech (High Quality)

Here are some important government schemes designed to support farmers. Click on the links to learn more and apply:

  • Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) – Direct income support of ₹6,000 annually to farmers.
  • Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) – Crop insurance for farmers against natural calamities.
  • Soil Health Card Scheme – Provides farmers with soil health reports and recommendations.
  • Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF) – Financial support for developing agricultural infrastructure.
  • National Agriculture Market (eNAM) – Online trading platform for farmers to sell their produce.

About AGRI दुनिया

Text-to-Speech
Our Story

Grown by Farmers. Delivered with Care.

Farmers put in the work to grow good food, but by the time it reaches a plate, a large share of its value has usually gone to people who never touched the soil. AGRI दुनिया exists to change that — a platform where farmers sell straight to the people who eat what they grow, and where farming feels less like a solo struggle and more like a shared effort.

What We Do

Direct Selling

Farmers list their produce and sell straight to consumers — no middlemen taking a cut along the way.

Order in Advance

Consumers place orders roughly 11–12 hours before delivery, depending on distance, so farmers know exactly what to harvest.

Organized Collection

Our assigned tempo and transport team collects produce in bulk directly from farmers, on schedule.

Fair Value, Both Sides

Cutting out unnecessary middlemen means better returns for farmers and better prices for consumers.

How We Work

Farmer Harvests

Fresh produce, harvested to match actual orders — not guesswork.

AGRI दुनिया Collects

Our tempo and transport team picks up produce in bulk from farmers.

Sorting & Distribution

Our team sorts the produce and prepares it for delivery.

Reaches Consumer

Fresh produce delivered to the buyer, at a fair price.

Beyond the Marketplace

AGRI दुनिया isn't only a place to buy and sell. We're working toward a cooperative-style farmer community, where farmers support each other instead of competing alone in an unorganized market.

  • Buy and sell farming equipment
  • Buy seeds and other agricultural resources
  • Learn modern farming techniques
  • Stay updated on newly launched government schemes
  • Follow more responsible, sustainable farming practices
  • Work together as a community, not in isolation

We're also working with farmers on agreements that encourage responsible use of agricultural chemicals and better farming practices — for healthier soil, and healthier food.

Our Vision

We want AGRI दुनिया to be more than an app — a farming community where produce moves straight from field to family, and where farmers have the tools, knowledge, and support to grow with confidence.

Whether you grow it or you buy it, there's a place for you here.
