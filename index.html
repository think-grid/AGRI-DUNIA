Government Schemes for Farmers

Text-to-Speech (High Quality)

Here are some important government schemes designed to support farmers. Click on the links to learn more and apply:

  • Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) – Direct income support of ₹6,000 annually to farmers.
  • Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) – Crop insurance for farmers against natural calamities.
  • Soil Health Card Scheme – Provides farmers with soil health reports and recommendations.
  • Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF) – Financial support for developing agricultural infrastructure.
  • National Agriculture Market (eNAM) – Online trading platform for farmers to sell their produce.
